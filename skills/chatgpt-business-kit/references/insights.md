# Insights: what the numbers mean

Unlocked after claim + Persona approval: Business Hub → Insights. 7-day and 28-day views; Brand and Agent tabs. Data includes activity from before you claimed and runs about a day behind.

| Metric | Definition (OpenAI) | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| **Mentions** | Online business: ChatGPT **references your website**; the brand name alone doesn't count. Local: ChatGPT shows a result for your location, even without a click. | How often you're a cited source |
| **Website clicks** | Recorded clicks on links to your site in ChatGPT. Doesn't confirm visits or conversions. | Compare with `utm_source=chatgpt.com` sessions in your analytics |
| **Profile views** | Shown on some dashboards; not defined by OpenAI | Treat as directional |
| **Discovery topics** | Common themes in answers that mentioned or cited you (top 5). No individual prompts. | Which pages to strengthen; topics you're missing = content gaps |

- **Blank ≠ zero.** Data appears only above an unpublished threshold. Stays blank for weeks → contact Support.
- No competitor or share-of-voice data in the beta.
- Launch-week dashboards [anecdotal]: 690 mentions → 24 clicks, and 28 → 1, so roughly 3–4 clicks per 100 mentions. Use this as a rough baseline, not a target.
- **Insights data is confidential** under OpenAI's terms: use it to measure and improve; agencies may see it under the same restrictions. Don't publish client dashboards, resell the data, or build ad audiences from it.

## Monthly routine (15 minutes)
1. Fill one row of `templates/insights-tracker.csv`.
2. Run the 10 buyer prompts from `crawlers-and-visibility.md`; log named/cited.
3. Pick one action: low mentions → add/strengthen the page answering a top buyer question; mentions but few clicks → make the cited page's first lines answer the question and give a reason to click; topics you don't want → fix the profile category/description.
