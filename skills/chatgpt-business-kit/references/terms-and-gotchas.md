# Read before you click: terms and gotchas
From OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT Terms, policies and help (12 Nov 2026) plus launch-week reports. Not legal advice.

## Terms worth knowing
- **Published profiles are permanent.** They can't be deleted, unpublished or hidden, even by Support. Only drafts can be deleted.
- **New profiles auto-publish** when Persona approves them. Write everything first.
- Your listing exists whether you claim it or not, and OpenAI may keep, add to, edit or replace it from other sources either way. Claiming lets you correct it.
- You give OpenAI a **permanent, irrevocable licence** to display the basic info you submit (name, address, contact, category, hours, website).
- **OpenAI may create and configure an agent for your business before you opt in**; it only goes live if published.
- **"OpenAI's AI assistant may make calls to you on behalf of our users."** Opt-out: realtime.chatgpt.com/stopcalls.
- You're responsible for your agent's content and outputs, even ones you never reviewed. OpenAI may remove agents at any time.
- Product feeds: allow OpenAI's crawlers and don't reserve data-mining opt-outs against OpenAI.
- Insights data is OpenAI's confidential information.
- No guarantee of placement, ranking, traffic or sales.
- Free in the beta; a paid model isn't ruled out.

## Gotchas
1. Claiming with a personal, freelancer or ex-employee account. The claimer becomes admin.
2. Thinking claim approval means done. Nothing is editable until Persona approves.
3. Phone/email codes go to the contact **on the listing**. Tracking numbers or old inboxes can't verify; use DNS.
4. Clicking "Verify domain" before the TXT record has propagated → "too many attempts" [anecdotal].
5. Name/address/registration mismatches → Persona failure with no explanation [anecdotal].
6. Shopify app verification loops or the wrong country stuck [anecdotal]: retry with the same ChatGPT account and browser tab, or verify by DNS instead.
7. Expecting brand-name mentions to count. Only website citations count for online businesses.
8. Reading a blank Insights panel as zero.
9. Cloudflare or another CDN blocking OpenAI's bots while robots.txt allows them.
10. Assuming the Business Hub agent is the one your ads use. It isn't, yet.
11. Agent instructions with urgency, guarantees or data collection → blocked or removed.
12. Confusing "ChatGPT Business" (a paid workspace plan) with "Businesses in ChatGPT" (free listings). You don't need the plan.
13. Expecting edits to appear instantly. OpenAI reviews them first.
14. Ads: only shown to Free and Go users; built in Ads Manager; ads don't change ChatGPT's answers.
